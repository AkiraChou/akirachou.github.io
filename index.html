Hi! Thanks for visiting.

I'm fascinated by how we make decisions, and how data can make them better. My interests sit where data engineering, applied AI, and product thinking overlap — I like using technology, and a bit of psychology, toward genuinely useful ends rather than optimization for its own sake.

Some things I'm chasing: building AI systems people can actually trust with real decisions, helping institutions run on evidence instead of instinct, and figuring out how a former econ major ends up debugging payment pipelines at 2am (still working on that one).
